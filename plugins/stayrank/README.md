# Stayrank

Stayrank scores an Airbnb listing out of 100 on seven axes (photos, title, description, amenities, reviews, price, trust), ranks the fixes by impact and writes the texts for you. This plugin brings the whole product into ChatGPT and Claude: you only visit stayrank.ai to create your account and to add listings to your balance.

## What you can do from the conversation

- **Add a listing** by pasting its Airbnb URL, or import every listing of a host profile.
- **Analyze it**: the plugin shows what the analysis will use from your balance and waits for your confirmation.
- **Read the report**: score, strengths, the fixes ranked by impact with ready-to-paste texts, the booking settings Airbnb says influence search, a comparison with the listings that rank in the same search, and which amenities your photos show or miss.
- **Work through the action plan**: get the exact text to copy and where to paste it on Airbnb, adjust it, mark it done.
- **Retouch your photos**: the whole listing in one go or photo by photo, with a preset or your own style. Retouches never change the room.
- **Manage your organization and team** (owners).

Stayrank never edits your Airbnb listing: you apply each change yourself, Stayrank tracks it. Stayrank is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Airbnb.

## Install

- **Claude**: add the connector `https://www.stayrank.ai/api/mcp` (Settings › Connectors › Add custom connector), or install this plugin from its marketplace, then sign in with your Stayrank account when asked.
- **ChatGPT**: Settings › Apps › create an app with the MCP server URL `https://www.stayrank.ai/api/mcp`, or install the Stayrank plugin from the directory once published.

Full guide: https://www.stayrank.ai/mcp
